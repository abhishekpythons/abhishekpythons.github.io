Bengaluru Space Expo 2026

Edge AI for space, under one watt

At the Bengaluru Space Expo 2026 we presented RisingCompute's edge AI work to Dr. V. Narayanan, Chairman of ISRO. Our demo ran a YOLOv8 object-detection model on a Microchip PolarFire FPGA at under 1 W. That is 30–40× more power-efficient than running the same model on a typical GPU, and the kind of power budget a small satellite can actually afford.
