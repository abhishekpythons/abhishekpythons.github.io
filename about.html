About

One mind, two halves.

The left half runs on orbits and equations. The right half runs on ideas, products and the occasional poem. Most of the interesting work happens in the bridge between them.

I'm Abhishek Verma, a space science and data science enthusiast. I graduated with a B.Tech in Avionics from IIST in 2025, and I'm now at the degree level of IIT Madras's online BS in Data Science & Applications. I've managed a satellite ground station, written the flight software for a payload now in orbit, started a company, and now help steer a student satellite mission. In November 2026 I'm travelling India on the Jagriti Yatra, while still finding time to build products and write verse.

04 · Right hemisphere

A little verse

Engineering tells you what is possible. Poetry reminds you why any of it matters.

Telemetry

Every ninety minutes she comes home,
a whisper over the horizon's rim —
ten minutes of voice, a burst of numbers,
then silence, and the waiting starts again.

Two degrees

By day I learned how things should fly,
by night how numbers learn to see;
somewhere between the wing and the dataset
is the person I am trying to be.
